# Contributing to SideCar for OpenMoxie

You do **not** need to be a programmer to help here. This project is run in a new,
AI-assisted way: **you bring the idea, the bug, the log, the snippet, or the finding —
and the technical work gets done from there.** A single good log line or a rough idea
can become a working feature.

## How it works

1. You post an **Issue** — a bug, an idea, or something you discovered.
2. The maintainer (a human) and the AI (Claude + Grok) study it. If it fits the
   project, the AI writes and tests the code.
3. The maintainer reviews it and merges it. **You get credited.**

The maintainer is the **final judge**, and support here is **AI-assisted**.

## Ways to help (most need no coding)

- **Report what happened.** Bugs, or "it worked once then stopped." Please include:
  your firmware version, whether OpenMoxie is native or Docker, the logs, the exact
  steps, and what you already tried. **Reports from a real robot are the most valuable
  thing here** — the AI has no Moxie of its own.
- **Share an idea in plain words.** "It would be great if Moxie could ___." How you
  imagine it working is enough.
- **Share a key snippet or a firmware finding.** A proto field, an address, a config
  value, a few lines of code, or a log line that reveals how something works. **You
  don't have to wire it into the project** — just describe it, and the AI takes care of
  the integration.
- **Test on your own robot.** Try a change and tell us what you see. Real-hardware
  testing catches what nothing else can (a bug once rebooted robots — a real-robot test
  is what caught it).
- **Improve the docs.** Tell us what was confusing, or suggest clearer wording.

## What makes a good issue

- Be specific: what you did, what you expected, what actually happened.
- Include versions, setup, logs, and exact steps where you can.
- For code or findings: **rough is fine.** A snippet or a description — not a finished
  pull request.

## A note on safety

This runs on a device made for children. Changes that affect the robot are tested on a
real Moxie before they ship. Please don't post anything that could harm a robot or a
child's data — and if a change feels risky, say so.

## How this project is maintained (honestly)

The code is written by AI (Claude + Grok), **directed and tested by the maintainer** —
who is not a programmer, but sets the goals, tests on the real robot, and decides what
ships. The maintainer has the final say on everything.

## Where to post

- **[Issues](../../issues)** — bugs, ideas, and findings.

Thank you. Even a rough idea or one log line is a real contribution here. 🙂
